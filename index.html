Cameras

The CCTV cameras we install

Six kinds of equipment cover most homes, offices and company premises. We help you choose, then install and connect each one.

Dome cameras

Discreet ceiling-mounted cameras for lobbies, offices and corridors.

Bullet cameras

Fixed cameras for gates, parking areas and building exteriors.

PTZ speed domes

Pan, tilt and zoom cameras that cover large open areas.

Wi-Fi home cameras

Easy-to-place indoor cameras for homes, viewed live from your phone.

DVR and NVR recorders

Recorders that store footage from your cameras so you can play it back.

Video door phones

See and speak to visitors from a screen inside your home or office.

Questions

CCTV questions we hear most

Can I watch my cameras from my phone?

Yes. We connect your cameras to the app on your phone and show you live view during handover.

How many cameras do I need?

It depends on your entrances, blind spots and the size of the space. We walk the property on a site visit and recommend the number and type of cameras.

Do the cameras work and record at night?

Night vision and recording options vary by model, so we match the camera model to your space and tell you what each one can do.

Which areas do you cover?

We install in Faridabad, Gurgaon and across Delhi NCR.

Can you add locks, sensors or access control too?

Yes. Forticam also installs smart locks, video doorbells, motion sensors, instant alerts and access control. See all services at forticam.in.
