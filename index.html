Services

Six ways Forticam protects your space

We install each of these on its own or as one system, so the doors, cameras and alerts in your space work together.

Smart Locks

Open the door with a fingerprint, PIN, card or phone. App-enabled models let you share access with family and visitors.

Video Doorbells

See and talk to visitors at your door from your smartphone.

Security Cameras

Watch live from your phone. Night vision and recording options vary by model, so we match the model to your space.

Motion Sensors

Detect movement and get alerts when someone enters a protected area.

Instant Alerts

Door, window and motion sensors send a notification to your phone or smart-home app when they are triggered.

Access Control

Manage who can enter with digital keys and access codes.

Cameras

CCTV cameras we install for homes and offices

We help you choose the right camera type for each area, then install and connect it to your phone.

Dome cameras

Discreet ceiling-mounted cameras for lobbies, offices and corridors.

Bullet cameras

Fixed cameras for gates, parking areas and building exteriors.

PTZ speed domes

Pan, tilt and zoom cameras that cover large open areas.

Wi-Fi home cameras

Easy-to-place indoor cameras for homes, viewed live from your phone.

DVR and NVR recorders

Recorders that store footage from your cameras so you can play it back.

Video door phones

See and speak to visitors from a screen inside your home or office.
